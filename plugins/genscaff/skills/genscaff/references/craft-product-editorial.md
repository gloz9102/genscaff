# Product Editorial Craft

## Selection

Select when the primary job is understanding a product through focused narrative and media. Do not select for comparison-heavy marketplaces, operational dashboards, or bounded transactions.

## Craft

- Give each section one message under one dominant visual idea.
- Use strong type hierarchy and progressive disclosure; media must explain the product.
- Keep the primary CTA continuous with the narrative, not detached decoration.
- Use motion only for hierarchy, continuity, or feedback, with a reduced-motion equivalent.
- Reorder media responsively for comprehension and tolerate long localized copy.

Typical patterns include an editorial hero, proof modules, feature explanations, media demonstrations, comparison facts, and a clear next action. Required states depend on real media and CTA behavior; consider slow/missing media, disabled autoplay, loading, success, and applicable failure. Decorative scroll effects are optional.

## Interaction, accessibility, and performance

Preserve reading and focus order across responsive rearrangement. Controls need names and visible focus; motion must respect user preference. Control image dimensions, responsive sources, LCP, layout stability, video payload, and autoplay. Verify desktop/mobile hierarchy, long copy, media fallback, CTA flow, keyboard/focus, reduced motion, and console/overflow.

Do not copy Apple composition, navigation, copy, typography, assets, or distinctive transitions.
