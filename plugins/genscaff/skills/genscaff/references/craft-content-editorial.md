# Content Editorial Craft

## Selection

Select when reading, scanning, navigating, or finding information is the primary job. Do not select for marketing narrative whose primary success is a product action.

## Craft

- Establish reading hierarchy, readable line measure, and responsive typography.
- Provide long-form navigation or a table of contents only when length justifies it.
- Pair media with captions and preserve metadata, references, footnotes, or annotations.
- Support long Korean and English text; evaluate RTL when localization applies.
- Add print or share behavior only when it serves the content task.

Watch wide tables, code, media, anchor offsets, fixed-height clipping, font loading, and long-document performance. Keyboard users must reach navigation, notes, and embedded controls in sensible order. Verify reading order, heading structure, landmarks, long-content reflow, table/media overflow, links, focus, and applicable print/share output.

Do not imitate a publisher's exact grid, typography, navigation, or branded editorial devices.
