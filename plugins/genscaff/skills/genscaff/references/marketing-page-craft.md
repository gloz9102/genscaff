# Marketing Page Craft

Use this after the router selects Marketing / landing. Preserve provided claims, campaign intent, and established brand evidence.

## Checks

- State the audience, proposition, and next action without relying on atmosphere alone.
- Keep each material claim next to its evidence, limitation, or source; do not invent proof.
- Give sections a deliberate progression from problem to evidence to action.
- Use one primary conversion path and make secondary paths visibly secondary.
- Keep visual demonstrations tied to the product or service result, not interchangeable decoration.
- Ensure CTA labels predict the destination and preserve campaign or form context.
- Treat testimonials, customer counts, certifications, integrations, and performance numbers as claims requiring provenance.
- On mobile, retain the proposition, decisive proof, and CTA before long supporting material.

## Claim ledger

- Classify each prominent statement as product fact, customer evidence, projection, or opinion.
- Link quantitative claims to a source and visible scope or time period.
- Remove placeholder logos, awards, ratings, and quotations when provenance is absent.
- Keep caveats readable instead of burying them in decorative fine print.

## Conversion continuity

- Verify the primary CTA opens the promised destination or next step.
- Preserve selected plan, campaign, referral, or form context across that transition.
- Provide recovery when a form, scheduler, or signup destination fails.
- Do not add competing CTAs merely to fill a repeated section pattern.

## Evidence

- Capture the proposition and decisive proof together at desktop and mobile where feasible.
- Exercise the primary CTA through its observable next state.
- Check the longest realistic claim, localization, and narrow viewport.
- Record which claims came from the user or repository and which remain unverified.

## Outcome gate

Score proposition clarity, claim evidence, section progression, brand coherence, and conversion continuity from 0 to 5 using `workflow-rubric.md`. Revise only dimensions below 3.
